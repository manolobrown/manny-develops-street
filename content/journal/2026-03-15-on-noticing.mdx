This is a placeholder essay until the real first one lands. Essays are slower than the Sunday letter — once a month at most, usually less — and they're where I think out loud about a frame, a series, or a question that took longer than a paragraph to work through.

## The format

A few hundred words, sometimes a few thousand. One lead image at the top, optional inline photos in the body, no more than is necessary. The serif body type, the warm paper background, the editorial rhythm — the whole brand is built for this layout, so essays inherit it without ceremony.

## What goes here

- Project notes from inside a series
- Behind-the-scenes for an editorial credit, after it ships
- Questions about technique or eye that won't fit in a caption
- Occasionally, a piece by an editor or photographer I'd reach out to specifically

What doesn't: news, listicles, "10 tips for street photography." The Sunday letter handles cadence; this handles depth.
